          07
          
Rules

          

            Principles, not a palette. The type, the colours and the tile are
            open; these are the things that would make the mark stop working.
          

        

        

          

            
Use

            
    
              
  • The constructed mark, not a CJK font glyph.
    • 
              
  • Any tile and glyph that clear 3:1 for a graphic, and 4.5:1 if the glyph is read as text.
    • 
              
  • A tile shape that holds the glyph: rounded, square or none.
    • 
              
  • An open font for the wordmark, self-hosted with its OFL if it is shipped.
    • 
              
  • Clear space of at least one stroke width.
    • 
              
  • The 16px PNG at 100% as the last check.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Do not

            
    
              
  • Set the shipped mark in a font. The icon generator has no font, and a font would make the icon differ per machine.
    • 
              
  • Use a brush 友, a red seal, a speech bubble, a flag or a translation arrow. They are the wrong register, and they collapse at 16.
    • 
              
  • Put IU inside the tile. The mark is 友; the wordmark is IU.
    • 
              
  • Recolour the icon per panel theme. The nine themes are the product, not the brand.
    • 
              
  • Add a gradient, shadow, bevel or outline.
    • 
              
  • Let the glyph and its ground fall below 3:1.
    • 
              
  • Stretch, rotate or crop the tile.
    • 
              
  • Put text inside the icon.
    •
