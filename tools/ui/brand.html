友

the mark

IU means I and you. 友 means friend in Mandarin, Japanese, Korean and Hokkien, which is where the name came from. The icon has to say that in 16 pixels, on a toolbar, without a Chinese font, and without becoming a generic character tile. This page is the workbench. The written case is in docs/BRAND-BOOK.md.

          06
          
Rules

          

            Ten rules that keep the mark from turning into a character tile, a
            language-app cliché, or a mark that only works at 128.
          

        

        

          

            
Use

            
    
              
  • The constructed mark, not a CJK font glyph.
    • 
              
  • The tile on the manifest icon, at 16, 32, 48 and 128.
    • 
              
  • White on brand blue as the primary; ink on paper as the editorial variant.
    • 
              
  • The wordmark in the interface sans, at 600 for IU and 400 for the descriptor.
    • 
              
  • Clear space of at least one stroke width on every side.
    • 
              
  • The 16px PNG at 100% as the final test, not the 512px SVG.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Do not

            
    
              
  • Set the mark in a font. The icon generator has no font, and a font would make the icon differ per machine.
    • 
              
  • Use a brush 友, a red seal, a speech bubble, a flag or a translation arrow. They are the wrong register and they collapse at 16.
    • 
              
  • Put IU inside the tile. The mark is 友; the wordmark is IU.
    • 
              
  • Recolour the icon per panel theme. The nine themes are the product, not the brand.
    • 
              
  • Add a gradient, shadow, bevel or outline.
    • 
              
  • Use the open blue glyph on paper: 2.87:1 is below the 3:1 non-text threshold.
    • 
            

          

        

      


      

        

          07
          
Decision

          

            This is a proposal, not a shipped change. The current icon in
            icons/ is still the IU monogram.
          

        

        

          

            The recommendation is Constructed: the 1.8px 友 on a
            rounded blue tile, white glyph, at every size. It is the only
            direction that stays original, survives 16px, and keeps the meaning
            of the name. The Paper and Seal variants are secondary expressions,
            not alternate manifests. The Open mark is for inline use on dark
            grounds.
          

          

            What is still open: whether the tile should stay blue or move to
            paper, whether the 1.8 stroke is too heavy at 32, and whether the
            wordmark should sit beside the mark or below it. The 16px row and the
            page are the review.
          

          

            The “Source glyph” is 友 typeset in WenQuanYi Zen Hei. It is on this
            page for comparison only, and the page relies on the machine having a
            CJK font. Do not copy it into icons/; the shipped mark must
            be the constructed path set.
