the mark

IU means I and you. 友 means friend in Mandarin, Japanese, Korean and Hokkien, which is where the name came from. The mark, the type and the colours are all open. This page is the decision tool: switch the direction, the font, the tile and the colours, and judge the actual 16.

          07
          
Rules

          

            Principles, not a palette. The type, the colours and the tile are
            open; these are the things that would make the mark stop working.
          

        

        

          

            
Use

            
    
              
  • The chosen 友 glyph as the logo, outlined from its OFL file for the icon.
    • 
              
  • The constructed four-stroke fallback if the face collapses at 16.
    • 
              
  • Any tile and glyph that clear 3:1 for a graphic, and 4.5:1 if the glyph is read as text.
    • 
              
  • A tile shape that holds the glyph: rounded, square or none.
    • 
              
  • Clear space of at least one stroke width.
    • 
              
  • The 16px PNG at 100% as the last check.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Do not

            
    
              
  • Ship a live font. The final icon must be the outlined glyph, or the constructed paths; the preview may use the font.
    • 
              
  • Assume a brush 友 survives 16px. It is a trial, not a default; use it only if the 16px sample still reads.
    • 
              
  • Use a red seal, a speech bubble, a flag or a translation arrow. They are the wrong register.
    • 
              
  • Put IU inside the tile. The mark is 友; the wordmark is IU.
    • 
              
  • Recolour the icon per panel theme. The nine themes are the product, not the brand.
    • 
              
  • Add a gradient, shadow, bevel or outline.
    • 
              
  • Let the glyph and its ground fall below 3:1.
    • 
              
  • Stretch, rotate or crop the tile.
    • 
              
  • Put text inside the icon.
    • 
            

          

        

      


      

        

          08
          
Decision

          

            Final for 0.1.0: Paper, Rounded Square, Paper and ink, Zen Kurenaido
            400. The icon in icons/ uses that outlined 友.
          

        

        

          

            The page opens at that state. Zen Kurenaido is the 0.1.0 face; it can be
            replaced with another face, weight, tile or colour, and the icon
            re-outlined for a future release. The wordmark is a descriptor in the UI font; the mark is
            the 友.
          

          

            The typeset preview relies on the live font. The icon does not: the
            glyph is outlined into icons/icon.svg, and the font file
            is not shipped. The constructed four-stroke drawing remains the
            fallback if the face fails at 16px.
          

          

            The book in docs/BRAND-BOOK.md records the 0.1.0 decision and
            the change procedure. The icon licence is in
            icons/OFL-ZenKurenaido.txt and the notices.
