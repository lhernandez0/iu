What this is showing
One lifecycle — auto-hide, no chevron
The bar fades after ~2.5s without pointer movement and returns on movement or on focus — tab into the faded bar and it comes back, which is the trap pointer-only hiding walks into. It does not hide while paused: pausing is what people do right before reaching for a control.
An error is shown, and the bar still fades
. The status line turns red and names the problem. It does NOT pin the bar open — a bar that refuses to get out of the way is its own annoyance, and the message stays in the DOM rather than being lost with the fade.
The cue is not in the bar
Status and errors only. The cue lives in the captions — having it in both places is exactly what made the toggle look inert.
Two rows, the second conditional
Transport always; the caption settings only while captions are on. Everything the reader needs, without a settings view and without a fifteen-button row.
Captions: over the picture or below it
Overlay is the default — standard subtitle position, costs no picture height. Below gives the strip its own row and the picture shrinks above it. Both are wanted, so it is a choice, and the switch is independent of the control bar: hide the controls and the captions stay.
Resume
shows the prompt. The position is stored against the file's identity; the file itself is re-picked, and the resume is checked against the stored descriptor so it cannot land in the wrong film.
